Pik Blink Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Pik Blink respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Pik Blink handles information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

Pik Blink does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use its features.

Data Storage

Pik Blink is designed to operate on your device. Information created or managed by the app remains on your device and is not sent to our servers.

Tracking and Advertising

Pik Blink does not use tracking technologies for advertising or cross-app tracking and does not display third-party advertising.

Third-Party Services

Pik Blink does not use third-party services to collect or process personal information.

Children's Privacy

Because Pik Blink does not collect personal information, the app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Pik Blink, please contact us through the contact information provided with the app.
